About The Hidden Chapter

The Hidden Chapter is a small book recommendation site and Facebook Page run by one person. The goal is simple: help you decide whether a book is worth your time.

How posts are made

We use AI tools to help research and draft posts. A person reviews every post before it goes up, checks the basic facts about the book, and edits it. We try to say who a book is not for, too.

What we don't do

  • We don't claim professional credentials we don't have, and nothing here is medical, mental-health, financial, or legal advice.
  • We don't invent personal stories or fake reviews.
  • We aren't paid by authors or publishers for recommendations.

How the site earns money

Some links go to Amazon and are affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. See the disclosure page for details.
