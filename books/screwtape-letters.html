What’s inside

Lewis imagines letters from the senior devil Screwtape to his nephew Wormwood. Through this inverted perspective, the story considers temptation and the ordinary habits of human life within a Christian frame.

A good fit for

Readers who enjoy fiction, satire, and questions about faith and character.

Worth knowing

The inverted voice can take some getting used to. Pick another book if you want direct instruction or a straightforward devotional.
