What’s inside

C. S. Lewis brings together arguments about morality, Christian belief, and Christian living. Adapted from his radio talks during the Second World War, the book presents an explanation and defense of the faith from his perspective.

A good fit for

Readers interested in Christian apologetics or an introduction to Lewis’s religious writing.

Worth knowing

This is an argument for Christian belief, rather than a neutral survey of world religions or a daily devotional.

Read it as one writer’s reasoned case, with space to question his assumptions and conclusions.
