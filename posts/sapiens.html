Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind tells the story of our species from early humans to the modern world, organized around a few major turning points.

What the book covers

  • The Cognitive Revolution and the role of shared stories and beliefs
  • The Agricultural Revolution and its trade-offs
  • How money, empires, and religions unified people
  • The Scientific Revolution and where it might lead

Who it's for

Curious readers who enjoy sweeping, idea-driven nonfiction. Some historians have criticized parts of the book for broad generalizations, so it pairs well with more detailed histories if a topic grabs you.
